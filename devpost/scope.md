# Hackathon Decoder

Paste a hackathon page and get three honest answers: Can I enter? When is my deadline in IST? What must I submit?

## The Unique Kernel
Do not guess. Every eligibility answer is **yes, no, or unsure**, always with the **exact line from the page** that decided it. If the page is unclear, or “about me” is missing, the app says **unsure** instead of inventing a yes.

## Who It's For
You, this week: a beginner with AI agents, juggling mid-sems and several hackathons with different deadlines and rules. Today you read a long page and try to work out whether you can enter, when it’s due, and what the submission actually requires. You want something unique, not a generic study app, and finishable in one afternoon.

## The Core Loop
Open the app → paste the text of one hackathon page → optionally type a short “about me” (for example `college student, India, solo`) → get three answers out. One pasted page in, three answers out. The “about me” text is used once and forgotten.

## Inspiration & Identity
Simple interface. Plain, honest, no extra chrome. Feels like a decoder, not a dashboard.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Practice planning a project properly before an agent builds it, be able to explain the code, and finish with a working app that can be demoed. Strongest in Java, also knows some Python.

## What "Working" Looks Like
For the demo, paste a real hackathon page read this week. On screen: eligibility with a quote (or unsure), deadline in IST with days left, and a short required-submit checklist separate from optional extras. The “oh, that’s cool” beat is **unsure + the exact quote**, not a fake confident yes.

## The POC Boundary
- One page pasted in, three answers out
- Optional single “about me” field; no profile, no login
- If “about me” is empty or the page doesn’t say, answer **unsure** and list who the page says can enter
- Smallest working version first

## Later
Deadline conversion to IST with days left — **only if time runs short**; then drop this and keep eligibility + required submit.

## Explicitly Cut
- Accounts, saving, or remembering “about me” — one afternoon, no login
- Calendar and reminders — not needed to prove the decoder
- Comparing several hackathons — one page in is the experiment
- Generic study or revision features — not this project
